## 답장 본문 (그대로 붙여넣기)

Hello, thank you for reviewing App Design Lab. Here is the requested information.

1. Screen recording
Attached. It was captured on a physical Mac running the latest macOS, starting from app launch. It shows the typical flow (opening a lesson and going through its five steps, the library, a lab, the quiz, the Notes tab) and how paid content is accessed (tapping a locked lesson, the purchase screen with the weekly, monthly and lifetime options, and Restore Purchases in Settings). The app has no account registration, login or account deletion, and no user-generated content that other users can see.

2. Purpose and target audience
App Design Lab is a hands-on workbook for learning UI/UX design decisions. Each lesson lets the learner use a sample screen that breaks one interface rule on purpose, write down what felt wrong, read the reasoning, compare it with the corrected screen, and keep a one-line takeaway. It also includes a reference library (68 topics, 100 short mistake retrospectives, 7 principles), 5 interactive labs and a 396-question quiz.
The audience is app developers, designers and students who want to understand why an interface decision is right, not just memorize rules. The problem it solves is that design guidance is usually read passively; here the learner feels the problem first and then sees the fix.

3. Setup and access
No setup, account or sample files are needed. Launch the app and pick a lesson in the Learn tab.
Free content: lessons 1 to 3, mistakes 1 to 10, the toolbar lab, quiz chapter 1, all 68 topics, the 7 principles and Notes.
Paid content: tap any locked lesson (lesson 4 or later) or open Settings to see the purchase screen. One purchase unlocks everything. Products: com.leeo.higgym.pro.weekly (auto-renewable), com.leeo.higgym.pro.monthly (auto-renewable), com.leeo.higgym.pro (non-consumable, lifetime). Optional tips: com.leeo.higgym.tip.small, .medium, .large (consumable, unlock nothing). Restore Purchases is in Settings. All of these products are already approved and on sale in the iOS version of this same app (same bundle ID, universal purchase).

4. External services
Apple StoreKit: in-app purchases and subscriptions.
Apple CloudKit (iCloud public database): the in-app feedback form sends the message the user writes to the developer, and anonymous usage statistics and crash diagnostics are sent without any personal identifier.
No third-party SDKs, no authentication service, no third-party payment processor, no advertising, and no AI service. All lessons and content are bundled in the app and work offline.

5. Regional differences
None. The app functions the same in every region. Only the interface language changes with the device language (24 languages).

6. Regulated industry or third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. All lesson text, mockups, sample screens and quiz questions are original work created by the developer. The app does not include third-party protected content.

Thank you.
